Section 01

💡 Problem Context & Product Strategy

🔴 The Traveler & Business Friction

  • Frequent Bus Delays: Private intercity buses encounter traffic jams, highway toll queues, and breakdown issues without real-time passenger updates.
  • Unannounced Boarding Point Shifts: Bus operators frequently change pickup points to avoid city congestion, stranding passengers.
  • High Support Operational Costs: Inbound calls to support cost ₹45+ per ticket, creating heavy operational margin drag.
  • Passenger Attrition: Passengers who experience unassisted delays have a 40% lower repeat booking rate.

🟢 The Product Solution (SmartRoute AI & Assist)

  • Predictive Anomaly Engine: ML model analyzing GPS telemetry & historical route speeds to detect delays up to 2 hours in advance.
  • Proactive Multichannel Messaging: Automated WhatsApp & Push alerts dispatched before passengers leave for the bus stand.
  • 1-Tap Automated Recovery: Free Express Cab dispatch for boarding shifts, instant ₹150 Wallet Snack Credit for >45m delays, and 0-fee rebooking.
  • Event-Driven Deflection Bot: Resolves 90% of location/ETA queries without human agent escalation.
